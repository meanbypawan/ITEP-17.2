Your Visualization Roadmap

For your ML preparation, I recommend learning the graphs in this order:

                 Matplotlib
                     ↓
          ┌──────────┴──────────┐
          ↓                     ↓
       Histogram             Boxplot
          ↓                     ↓
    Distribution             Outliers
          │                     │
          └──────────┬──────────┘
                     ↓
                 Scatter
                     ↓
                Correlation
                     ↓
               Heatmap
                     ↓
               Bar / Count
                     ↓
                  Seaborn
                     ↓
             Multivariate EDA

The most important graphs for your ML journey are:

Graph	Main purpose
Histogram	Distribution
Boxplot	Outliers + distribution
Scatterplot	Relationship between numerical variables
Barplot	Comparing categories
Countplot	Category frequency
Heatmap	Correlation
Line plot	Time-series/tren
